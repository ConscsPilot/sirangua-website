What Sirangua is

Sirangua is an environmental sensing and interpretation platform designed to help gardeners, farmers, and researchers understand the complex relationships between plants, light, soil, and weather. By combining sensor data with solar modeling and spatial analysis, Sirangua provides insights into how environmental factors influence plant growth and health.

What's being built

  • Sensor-based environmental monitoring
  • Solar and light modeling by location and orientation
  • Tools for understanding plant–environment interactions
